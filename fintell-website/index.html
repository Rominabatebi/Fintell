AI and machine learning, explained

Where Fintell uses AI, and where it deliberately doesn't.

Four layers turn raw files into decisions. Each layer uses the technique that fits the job, and each is labelled with what is built today.

1

Understand the data

RulesBuilt

The AI Data Studio recognises columns in uploaded files using a dictionary of finance terms and synonyms, scores its confidence for each match, flags missing fields and cleans formats automatically.

2

Forecast with machine learning

Machine learningBuilt

Statistical models (ETS, ARIMA, Prophet and others) run next to machine-learning models. Random Forest and XGBoost learn how monthly growth follows recent growth (the last two months and a three-month average) and forecast one month at a time. Economic drivers (GDP growth, inflation and interest rates) enter through the economic-driver and stress models. Machine-learning models need about 24 months of history to be reliable.

Every model is back-tested on months it never saw and ranked by error (MAPE). A best-model ensemble averages the strongest models, which is often more stable than trusting one.

3

Simulate risk

SimulationBuilt

Monte Carlo simulation runs hundreds to thousands of possible futures with uncertain growth and economic conditions, so you see a range and a probability, not just one line.

4

Explain in plain language

Generative AIRule-based today · LLM planned

Today, a rule engine writes the CEO brief and answers in the AI CFO chat from the computed figures. Next, a large language model will answer questions with retrieval over the company's own documents, and must cite the exact table rows and formulas it used.

Ask the AI CFO · sample company
Can we afford to hire two more people this year?
Yes, with care. Today you have €435,000 in cash and 12.1 months of runway. Two hires at about €4,500 a month each would lower runway to roughly 9.6 months. That is still above the 9-month warning line, but it leaves less room if revenue growth slows. A safer path is to hire one person now and the second once monthly revenue passes €140,000.

Sample answer from the prototype's rule-based assistant. The production version grounds a language model in the same computed figures.

Forecast back-testing

Which forecast should you trust?

Fintell hides the last months of your history, lets each model forecast them, and ranks the models by error. The prototype runs 11 models, from ETS, ARIMA and Prophet to the machine-learning models Random Forest and XGBoost. This browser version runs five statistical models on real numbers.

Sample: the prototype's demo company, monthly revenue Jan–Dec 2024. Paste at least 8 of your own monthly figures to test them.

Cost of capital · WACC

What return must the business earn?

WACC blends the cost of equity (CAPM) with the after-tax cost of debt. Betas, risk premia, synthetic credit ratings and spreads come from the Fintell prototype's valuation tables; the risk-free rate comes from the ECB.

Currency exposure

What could exchange rates do to your numbers?

For revenue, costs or investments outside the euro area: today's ECB rate and the currency's measured volatility over the last year give a range for the value in euros.

Diligence scorecard

Do the numbers hold up?

Six checks investors run on every SaaS deal, with the questions to ask the founder when something looks off.

Result

Questions to ask the founder

    Thresholds are common venture rules of thumb, not standards. Burn multiple = net cash burned ÷ net new ARR.

    Price check · VC method

    Is the asking price compatible with your return target?

    Works backwards from a realistic exit: revenue at exit × public market multiple, discounted for being private, then divided by the return you need.

    Deal returns

    What will your ticket really return?

    Follows your ownership through future rounds and the option pool, then compares the return with inflation and the risk-free rate.

    Portfolio simulator

    How likely is your portfolio to make money?

    Early-stage returns follow a power law: most companies fail and a few carry the fund. This simulates 4,000 portfolios from the outcome mix you set.
